How to play

Win by knockout (HP to 0) or a 3-count pin on an opponent under 25% HP. You only need the arrow keys and 3 buttons.

  • Attack punches up close and kicks from a step away. Tap it 3 times fast for a combo. While running it's a running strike; on a downed opponent it's a stomp.
  • Grab throws a standing opponent and pins a downed one. Press it just before they grab you to reverse their throw.
  • Special does your signature move at 50 meter and your finisher at 100. Hits fill the meter.
  • Press ↑ in a corner to climb, then Attack to dive.
  • When pinned, mash any button to kick out. Below 25% HP it gets harder.
